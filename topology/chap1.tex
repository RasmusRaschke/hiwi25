\section{The Theorem of Seifert-van Kampen}
We start with one of the most important theorems to calculate $\pi_1$, especially if one has a topological space given as a quotient, i.e. a fundamental square.
\begin{theorem}[Seifert-van Kampen]
   Let $X$ be a path-connected topological space such that $X = X_1 \cup X_2$ with $X_1,X_2$ open in $X$ and $x_0 \in X_1 \cap X_2 =:X_0$. Furthermore, let $X_0,X_1,X_2$ be path-connected. Then there is an isomorphism of fundamental groups:
   \begin{equation}
       \pi_1(X_1,x_0) \star_{\pi_1(X_0,x_0)} \pi_1(X_2,x_0) \to \pi_1(X,x_0).
   \end{equation}
\end{theorem}

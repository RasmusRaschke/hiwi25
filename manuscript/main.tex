\section{Equations of Motion}
Consider an infinite uncurved hyperplane $H \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3$ of slope $s \in \mathbb{R}$ and a rigid solid sphere of radius $R$ and mass $m$ in contact with the plane. Additionally, the sphere behaves as ideal magnetic dipole with dipole moment $\bm{\mu}_0$. The configuration manifold of the sphere is $Q := H \times \SO(3) \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3 \times \SO(3)$, and hence the velocity phase space is 
$$TQ=TH \times T\SO(3) \cong \mathbb{R}^2 \times \so(3) \cong \mathbb{R}^2 \times \mathbb{R}^3 \subseteq \mathbb{R}^6.$$
Choose two global frames of $\mathbb{R}^3$, one constant frame $\{\bm{e}_x,\bm{e}_y,\bm{e}_z\}$ whose origin defines the position of the sphere at $t=0$, and one moving frame $\{\hat{\bm{e}}_x(t), \hat{\bm{e_y}}(t), \hat{\bm{e_z}}(t)\}$ attached to the center of mass (COM) of the sphere. We will first derive the general equations of motions in $\mathbb{R}^3 \times \SO(3)$ followed by transforming the equations to an $H$-adapted basis. All vectors measured with respect to the COM-frame will be denoted with a hat.
